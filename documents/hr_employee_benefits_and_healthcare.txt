IntellectBridge Employee Benefits and Healthcare Guide

1. Health Insurance Coverage
Our comprehensive health insurance plan covers 100% of preventive care, including annual physical exams, vaccinations, and screenings. Standard medical treatments, specialist visits, and emergency room visits are covered at 90% after meeting a $500 individual deductible.

2. Dental and Vision Care
- Dental Plan: Covers 100% of preventive cleanings (twice per year) and 80% of basic restorative procedures (fillings, extractions). Orthodontic treatments are covered at 50%, capped at a lifetime maximum of $2,000 per dependent.
- Vision Plan: Reimburses up to $200 per year for prescription eyeglasses or contact lenses. It also covers one annual comprehensive eye exam at 100% with no copay.

3. Mental Health and Wellness
IntellectBridge provides an Employee Assistance Program (EAP) which offers 6 free, confidential counseling sessions per year with certified therapists for employees and their immediate dependents.

4. Pension and Retirement Match
The company matches 100% of employee retirement contributions up to 5% of their base salary. Employees are 100% vested in the company matching contributions immediately upon enrollment in the plan.
