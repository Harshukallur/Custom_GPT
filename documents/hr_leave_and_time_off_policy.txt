IntellectBridge Corporate Leave and Time Off Policy

1. Annual Paid Leave (Vacation)
All full-time employees are entitled to 25 business days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Vacation days accrue at a rate of 2.08 days per month. A maximum of 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next calendar year, and must be used by March 31st of that year, or they will be forfeited.

2. Sick Leave
Employees receive up to 10 fully paid sick leave days per year for personal illness or medical appointments. If sick leave exceeds 3 consecutive days, a formal medical certificate signed by a licensed physician must be submitted to HR.

3. Parental Leave
- Maternity Leave: Eligible female employees are entitled to 16 weeks of fully paid maternity leave. This can begin up to 4 weeks before the expected delivery date.
- Paternity Leave: Eligible partners are entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid paternity leave, which must be taken within the first 12 months following the child's birth or adoption.

4. Bereavement Leave
Employees are granted up to 5 consecutive paid days of leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, or sibling).
