IntellectBridge Business Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy

1. Flight and Train Booking
All business travel bookings must be made through the corporate travel portal at least 14 days in advance. 
- Flights under 6 hours must be booked in Economy Class.
- Flights exceeding 6 consecutive hours are eligible for Premium Economy or Business Class booking, subject to prior manager approval.

2. Hotel Accommodation
The maximum allowable nightly rates for hotels (excluding taxes) are capped by location:
- Tier 1 Cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo): $250 per night.
- Tier 2 Cities (Chicago, Paris, Sydney, Munich): $180 per night.
- All other locations: $120 per night.

3. Meals and Incidentals (Per Diem)
Employees are reimbursed for actual meal expenses incurred during travel, up to a maximum daily limit (per diem cap) of $75. Receipts are required for all individual transactions exceeding $25. No reimbursement is provided for alcoholic beverages.

4. Personal Vehicle Usage
If an employee uses their personal vehicle for business purposes, they are reimbursed at the standard mileage rate of $0.67 per mile. Tolls and parking fees are fully reimbursable with receipts, but traffic violations or parking tickets are not eligible.
