IntellectBridge IT Security and Remote Work Policy

1. Work From Home (Remote Work) Eligibility
Employees whose roles permit remote work may work from home up to 3 days per week, with Tuesday and Thursday designated as core in-office collaboration days. Employees must maintain a secure, quiet, and professional workspace with a reliable high-speed internet connection.

2. Device and Network Security
- Employees must only use company-issued laptops and devices to access corporate systems. Personal computers are strictly prohibited.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) must be enabled on all accounts.
- VPN Usage: The secure corporate VPN must be active at all times when connecting to the internet from outside the office.
- Under no circumstances should corporate devices be connected to public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks without active VPN protection.

3. Password Management
Passwords must be a minimum of 12 characters and include a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special symbols. Passwords must be changed every 90 days, and reuse of the last 5 passwords is prohibited.

4. Phishing and Security Incident Reporting
Any suspicious emails, unexpected MFA prompts, or potential security breaches must be reported immediately to the IT Security Response Team at security@intellectbridge.com or by clicking the "Report Phishing" button in Outlook.
